Pen Tool Extras

This script can break at any time. It relies on SudokuPad's internals, which any update can change, and it isn't made or supported by SudokuPad.

Adds to SudokuPad's pen tool: customizable pen colors, dashed and arrow lines, and marks on cell edges and grid corners. Turn the pen tool on in SudokuPad's settings first.

What it adds

  • Customizable pen colors

    Hold a pen color button, or pick a preset in the settings.

  • Dashed and arrow lines

    Two more pages of pens.

  • Marks on cell edges

    Click an edge for an X, a circle or an arrow.

  • Marks on grid corners

    Click near a corner for an X or a circle.

  • Erase a whole line

    Right-click a line to remove all of it, or a mark to remove that.

  • White outlines

    An outline keeps lines readable over colored cells.

The pen panel

Pick SudokuPad's pen tool and its panel opens beside the puzzle. It holds every control.

  1. Pick a color. Click a pen color button. Hold one to put a color picker over each button and change it.
  2. Pick a line type. The line type button turns the pages of pens: solid, dashed and arrow.
  3. Pick a line mode. The line mode button snaps lines to cell centers, edges or both.
  4. Set thickness and opacity. The two sliders change how thick and how solid the next line or mark is.
  5. Open the settings. The settings button opens the dialog with every pen option.

Good to know

  • Others see your dashed and arrow lines only if they have the script too.
