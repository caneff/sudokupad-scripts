Pen Tool Extras

This script can break at any time. It relies on SudokuPad's internals, which any update can change, and it isn't made or supported by SudokuPad.

Adds to SudokuPad's pen tool: customizable pen colors, dashed and arrow lines, and marks on cell edges and grid corners. Turn the pen tool on in SudokuPad's settings first.

Install Pen Tool Extras

What it adds

  • Customizable pen colors. Hold a pen color button, or pick a preset in the settings.
  • Dashed lines and arrow lines. Two more pages of pens.
  • Marks on cell edges. Click an edge for an X, a circle or an arrow. Right-click goes back.
  • Marks on grid corners. Click near a corner for an X or a circle.
  • Erase a whole line. Middle-click a line to remove all of it.
  • White outlines. An outline keeps lines readable over colored cells.

The pen panel

Good to know

  • Others see your dashed and arrow lines only if they have the script too.
