Pen Tool Extras

This script can break at any time, and there are no guarantees. It relies on SudokuPad's internals, which aren't a public API, so any SudokuPad update can break it without notice. It isn't made or supported by SudokuPad.

A userscript that adds colors, extra pens, sliders and edge and corner marks to SudokuPad's pen tool. Turn the pen tool on in SudokuPad's settings first.

Install Pen Tool Extras

Features

  • Pen colors: nine colors that match your cell colors; change them in the settings dialog, with a button there for SudokuPad's own pen colors, or by holding a pen color button.
  • Three pen pages: 27 pens, solid, dashed and arrow lines; click the page button, press Tab or turn the mouse wheel over the page button to change page. Either extra page can be switched off in the settings dialog.
  • Two sliders: Thickness and Opacity, in the pen panel (each can be hidden there), and the length of the dashes and the spacing of the arrows, in the settings dialog.
  • White outline: a white outline round every pen mark, on by default, with a width slider, set in the settings dialog.
  • Edge marks: click a cell edge to cycle an X, a circle and direction arrows.
  • Corner marks: click near a grid corner to cycle an X and a circle.
  • Middle click erases a line: the whole line, branches and all, in one undo step; arrow lines are one line from either direction. It can be switched off in the settings dialog.
  • Right click goes backwards: on an edge or a corner it steps the cycle back.

Good to know

  • Others can't see lines from pens past the first page unless they have the script too.
