Pen Tool Extras

This script can break at any time. It relies on SudokuPad's internals, which any update can change, and it isn't made or supported by SudokuPad. When an update breaks one part, only that part turns off, and a bar at the bottom of the page names it with a link to report it.

Adds to SudokuPad's pen tool: customizable pen colors, dashed and arrow lines, and marks on cell edges and grid corners. Turn the pen tool on in SudokuPad's settings first.

What it adds

  • Customizable pen colors

    Hold a pen color button, or pick a preset in the settings.

  • Dashed and arrow lines

    Two more pages of pens.

  • Digit keys pick the pen

    Press 1 to 9 to pick that pen color, on whichever page of pens is showing.

  • Plus and minus keys set the thickness

    Press + or - to make lines and marks one step thicker or thinner, as the Thickness slider does.

  • Marks on cell edges

    Click an edge for an X, a circle or an arrow.

  • Marks on grid corners

    Click near a corner for an X or a circle.

  • Marks in cells

    Click the center of a cell for a circle, a cross or a dot.

  • Erase a whole line

    Right-click a line to remove all of it, or a mark to remove that.

  • White outlines

    An outline keeps lines readable over colored cells.

The pen panel

Pick SudokuPad's pen tool and its panel opens beside the puzzle. It holds every control.

Good to know

  • Others see your dashed and arrow lines only if they have the script too.
