Draw Tool

This script can break at any time. It relies on SudokuPad's internals, which any update can change, and it isn't made or supported by SudokuPad.

Adds to SudokuPad a Draw tool for freehand lines, shapes and typed text, such as cage sums, in colors of its own.

What it adds

  • Freehand lines

    Drag on the puzzle to draw. Hold Shift for a straight line.

  • Shapes

    Rectangle or ellipse, drawn in exactly the box you drag.

  • Text

    Click without dragging to type, such as a cage sum. Enter or a click elsewhere finishes it, Escape drops it, and a click on text changes it.

  • Colors of its own

    Nine colors that belong to Draw Tool, not to SudokuPad's pen. Pick one with a click or the digit keys 1 to 9.

  • Size and opacity

    Two sliders. A line, shape or text keeps the size and opacity it was drawn with.

  • Erasing

    Drag with the right mouse button across a line, shape or text to erase it. Clear all, in the panel, erases everything.

  • Settings

    Color presets, a color picker for each of the nine colors, and Copy and Paste to move them to another browser.

  • Stays on the cells

    Drawings stay put, and stay visible when you switch back to entering digits.

  • Kept for each puzzle

    A reload keeps the drawing, for the last 50 puzzles you drew on.

The Draw panel

Pick the Draw tool among SudokuPad's tool buttons and its panel opens beside the puzzle. It holds every control.

  1. Pick a color. Click a color button or press 1 to 9. Hold a button to put a color picker over each and change it.
  2. Pick a shape. The Shape button steps Off, rectangle and ellipse. With a shape on, a drag draws it.
  3. Set size and opacity. Size sets the width of the next line or shape and the size of the next text. Opacity sets how solid it is.
  4. Clear all. Erases the whole drawing, after it asks.
  5. Open the settings. The gear, or the Experimental settings entry, opens a dialog with color presets (the pen colors, three darker page sets and SudokuPad's colors), a color picker for each color, and Copy settings and Paste settings.

Good to know

  • The drawing is kept in this browser only, so another browser or device does not show it.
  • Restart clears the drawing. If a drawing cannot be saved, a message says so.
