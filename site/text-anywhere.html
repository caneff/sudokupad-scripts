Text Anywhere

This script can break at any time. It relies on SudokuPad's internals, which any update can change, and it isn't made or supported by SudokuPad. When an update breaks one part, only that part turns off, and a bar at the bottom of the page names it with a link to report it.

Adds to SudokuPad a Text Anywhere tool for typed notes on the board or beside it, such as cage sums, in colors of its own.

Install Text Anywhere

Left click to type a note

Background: a box behind the next note

Select: drag a box round notes, then change them together

Long notes scroll; drag any corner to resize

Erase: right-click a note

What it adds

  • Typed notes anywhere

    Click to type; Esc or Ctrl+Enter finishes; Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y undo and redo the typing; double-click a note to edit.

  • Formatting

    Discord-style bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, headers, subtext, bullets, numbered and checklist items, and code, and ->, != or sum typed as → ≠ Σ. One note below shows every style, and the text that made it.

  • Long notes

    Wraps and scrolls; drag a corner to resize.

  • Readable over digits

    A white outline round the letters, and an optional box behind them.

  • Colors, size and opacity

    Nine colors of its own (keys 1-9), and Size and Opacity sliders. The + and - keys make the selected notes one Size step bigger or smaller. A size, opacity or color you set also becomes the setting for the next note.

  • Select and move

    Click a note or drag a box round several; drag to move, arrow keys nudge.

  • Key to switch on

    Press T to pick the Text tool (not while you are typing in a note, a dialog or the Letter tool).

  • Hide all notes

    The eye beside the sliders hides every note to show the board, and shows them again. Placing a note shows them too, and a reload does.

  • Erase

    Right-click a note, or press Delete.

  • Kept per puzzle

    Notes survive a reload, for your last 50 puzzles.

  • Settings

    Color presets, font (proportional or fixed width), halo, box color and border by sample; Copy and Paste to another browser.

One note, every style

Each style in the finished note (above) and the typed text that made it (below). Click a checklist box to tick it.

The Text Anywhere panel

Pick the Text tool and its panel opens beside the puzzle.

Good to know

  • The notes are kept in this browser only, so another browser or device does not show them.
  • Undo right after Restart restores the notes. If the notes cannot be saved, a message says so.
