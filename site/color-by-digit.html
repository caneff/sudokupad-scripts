Color by Digit

This script can break at any time. It relies on SudokuPad's internals, which any update can change, and it isn't made or supported by SudokuPad. When an update breaks one part, only that part turns off, and a bar at the bottom of the page names it with a link to report it.

Adds a button to SudokuPad that colors cells, or the digits in them, by which group each digit is in, and keeps the colors up to date as you solve.

What it adds

  • Four modes

    Each press of the button moves to the next mode: Cell, Digit, Both, Off. The word above the chips names the mode.

  • Updates as you solve

    A cell takes its group's color when its digit, or every one of its center marks, is in that group. The color goes when that stops being true.

  • Colored digits

    In Digit and Both, digits and marks take a darker shade of their group's color. A mark that conflicts gets a red circle.

  • Profiles

    A profile splits the digits into groups and gives each a color. Five come built in (Low/5/High, Odd/Even, Entropic, Modular and Each digit), and you can keep up to six.

  • Quick switching

    Click the arrow on the button, or right-click or hold the button, to open the profile list. A pick applies at once.

  • A separate digit profile

    Digits can follow the cell profile, use another profile, or go uncolored.

The settings dialog

Edit profiles, in the profile menu, opens it.

A click on a digit already in the chosen color clears it.

From the keyboard, Tab and the arrow keys move around the colors and the digit keys, and typing 0 to 9 paints that digit.

Good to know

  • A digit or center mark change and its color change are one undo step. Undo, redo and replays never color, but undoing or redoing a mode change that colored or cleared cells moves the button to the mode it had then.
  • With SudokuPad's Multi-color setting off, a cell holds one color, so a group's color replaces any other color the cell had.
  • The button sits in the bottom row with Undo, Redo, Check and Select. That row holds five buttons, and any more, from this script or another, wrap onto a second row under it.
