Color by Digit

This script can break at any time. It relies on SudokuPad's internals, which any update can change, and it isn't made or supported by SudokuPad. When an update breaks one part, only that part turns off, and a bar at the bottom of the page names it with a link to report it.

Colors cells, or the digits and marks in them, by digit, with colors you give to groups of digits, and keeps the colors in step as you solve.

What it adds

  • On and off

    A press of the button turns coloring on or off. The Cell and Digit checkboxes at the top of the profile menu choose what it colors, cells, digits or both, and the word above the chips names it: Cell, Digit, Both, or Off. With only Digit ticked, a press leaves the colors you painted yourself alone.

  • Updates as you solve

    A cell takes its group's color when its digit, or every one of its center marks, is in that group. The color goes when that stops being true.

  • Colored digits

    In Digit and Both, digits and marks take a darker shade of their group's color. A mark that conflicts gets a red circle.

  • Profiles

    A profile splits the digits into groups and gives each a color. Five come built in (Low/5/High, Odd/Even, Entropic, Modular and Each digit), and you can keep up to six.

  • Quick switching

    Click the arrow on the button, or right-click or hold the button, to open the profile list. A pick applies at once, and the Cell and Digit checkboxes above it apply at once too and keep the list open.

  • A separate digit profile

    Digits can follow the cell profile or use another profile.

The settings dialog

Edit profiles, in the profile menu, opens it.

A click on a digit already in the chosen color clears it.

Restore defaults brings back the five preloaded profiles and the Cell and Digit pulldowns' starting picks and ticks On and Cell and unticks Digit, after asking in the dialog when it would remove your own profiles. Save keeps them and Cancel drops them.

From the keyboard, the colors and the digit keys are one Tab stop each, the arrow keys move within them, and typing 0 to 9 paints that digit.

Good to know

  • A digit or center mark change and its color change are one undo step. Undo, redo and replays never color, but undoing or redoing a press or a Save that colored or cleared cells moves the button and its checkboxes back to what they were then, and undoing or redoing a profile switch or a Save that recolored the cells moves the active profile too, and the groups the Save changed.
  • With SudokuPad's Multi-color setting off, a cell holds one color, so a group's color replaces any other color the cell had.
  • The button sits in the bottom row with Undo, Redo, Check and Select. That row holds five buttons, and any more, from this script or another, wrap onto a second row under it.
