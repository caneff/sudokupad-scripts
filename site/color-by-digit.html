Color by Digit

This script can break at any time. It relies on SudokuPad's internals, which any update can change, and it isn't made or supported by SudokuPad.

Adds to SudokuPad a button that colors cells, or the digits and marks in them, by digit, with colors you give to groups of digits, and keeps the colors in step as you solve.

Install Color by Digit

What it adds

  • Four modes. A press on the button steps the mode Cell → Digit → Both → Off, and the button's top strip names it.
  • Kept in step as you solve. A cell holding one group's digit, or with center marks that all fall in one group, takes that group's color, and loses it when the digit or marks change.
  • Digit and mark shades. In Digit and Both, entered digits, corner marks and center marks take a darker shade of their group's color. A mark that conflicts keeps its shade and gets a small red circle.
  • Profiles. A profile is a list of groups of digits, each group with a palette color, and you can keep up to six profiles. Five come preloaded: Low/5/High, Odd/Even, Entropic, Modular and Each digit. The button's icon shows the active profile.
  • The profile menu. It opens from the arrow box on the button, a right click or a hold. Pick a profile and it takes effect at once.
  • Sync digit profile with cell profile. Ticked, one profile colors both the cells and the digits. Untick it to color the cells from one profile and the digits from another; the menu then has a Cells section and a Digits section.
  • Use digit colors. Untick it to drop digit coloring: the button steps only between Cell and Off.

The settings dialog

Edit profiles in the menu opens it. It lists the profiles, which you reorder, delete and add a copy of; click a profile to edit it and rename it on the right, and use the small tags on each row to choose which profile colors the cells and which the digits (one Active tag while Sync is ticked). The right column edits the selected profile's groups, each one short row with its color and its digits; click a row to open that group's color and digit pickers (one group is open at a time). In a group's digit pad, a digit another group holds shows that group's color as a dot, and a click moves it. The two checkboxes above sit in a row across the top, with the mode as one row of buttons under them. On a desktop the dialog is two columns, the profile list on the left and the groups editor on the right; on a phone it is one, the editor under the list. Copy settings and Paste settings, in the bottom row beside Cancel and Save, move all of it to another browser. Save applies every change at once; Cancel drops them.

Good to know

  • A digit or center mark change and its color change are one undo step. Undo, redo and replays never color.
  • With SudokuPad's Multi-color setting off, a cell holds one color, so a group's color replaces any other color the cell had, and undoing a profile switch does not bring that color back.
