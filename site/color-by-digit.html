Color by Digit

This script can break at any time. It relies on SudokuPad's internals, which any update can change, and it isn't made or supported by SudokuPad.

Adds to SudokuPad a button that colors cells, or the digits and marks in them, by digit, with colors you give to groups of digits, and keeps the colors in step as you solve.

What it adds

  • Four modes

    A press on the button steps Cell, Digit, Both and Off. Its chips show how the mode colors the board.

  • Kept in step as you solve

    A cell takes its group's color when its digit, or all its center marks, fall in one group, and loses it when they change.

  • Digit and mark shades

    In Digit and Both, digits and marks take a darker shade of their group's color. A conflicting mark keeps its shade and gets a red circle.

  • Profiles

    A profile is a list of digit groups, each with a color. Keep up to six; five come preloaded.

  • The profile menu

    Click, right click or hold the button's arrow box. Pick a profile and it takes effect at once.

  • Cells and digits apart

    The Digit pulldown follows the cell profile, takes another profile, or is None to drop digit coloring.

The settings dialog

Edit profiles, in the profile menu, opens it.

  1. Choose what colors what. The pulldowns set the Mode, the Cell profile and the Digit profile.
  2. Pick a profile to edit. Click a row in the list. You can also rename, reorder, delete or copy it.
  3. Paint the groups. Click a swatch, then click or drag across digits to give them its color. A click on a digit already in that color clears it.
  4. Save. Save applies every change at once. Cancel drops them. Copy settings and Paste settings, top right, move everything to another browser.

Good to know

  • A digit or center mark change and its color change are one undo step. Undo, redo and replays never color.
  • With SudokuPad's Multi-color setting off, a cell holds one color, so a group's color replaces any other color the cell had.
  • The button sits in the bottom row with Undo, Redo, Check and Select, or in a second row under it once that row holds five or more buttons. It never moves another script's button.
