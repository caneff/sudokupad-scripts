Color by Digit

This script can break at any time. It relies on SudokuPad's internals, which any update can change, and it isn't made or supported by SudokuPad.

Adds to SudokuPad a button that colors cells, or the digits and marks in them, by digit, with colors you give to groups of digits, and keeps the colors in step as you solve.

Install Color by Digit

What it adds

  • Four modes. A press on the button steps the mode Cell → Digit → Both → Off, and the button's top strip names it.
  • Kept in step as you solve. A cell holding one group's digit, or with center marks that all fall in one group, takes that group's color, and loses it when the digit or marks change.
  • Digit and mark shades. In Digit and Both, entered digits, corner marks and center marks take a darker shade of their group's color. A mark that conflicts keeps its shade and gets a small red circle.
  • Profiles. A profile is a list of groups of digits, each group with a palette color, and you can keep up to six profiles. Five come preloaded: Low/5/High, Odd/Even, Entropic, Modular and Each digit. The button's icon shows the active profile.
  • The profile menu. It opens from the arrow box on the button, a right click or a hold. Pick a profile and it takes effect at once.
  • Digit: Same as cell. The settings dialog's Digit pulldown starts here: one profile colors both the cells and the digits. Pick a profile by name to color the cells from one profile and the digits from another; the menu then has a Cells section and a Digits section.
  • Digit: None. Pick it to drop digit coloring: the button steps only between Cell and Off.

The settings dialog

Edit profiles in the menu opens it. At the top are three pulldowns: Mode, Cell (the profile that colors the cells) and Digit (Same as cell, a profile, or None). Under them, on a desktop, are two columns, the profile list on the left and the selected profile's groups on the right; on a phone the pulldowns and the columns each stack.

The profile list is only for editing: click a row to edit that profile's colors, rename it in its row, reorder, delete, or add a copy of it. The pulldowns pick which profile colors the cells and which the digits. In an open group's digit pad, a digit another group holds shows that group's color as a dot, and a click moves it. The two icons at the right end of the title row, Copy settings and Paste settings, move all of it to another browser. Save applies every change at once; Cancel drops them.

Good to know

  • A digit or center mark change and its color change are one undo step. Undo, redo and replays never color.
  • With SudokuPad's Multi-color setting off, a cell holds one color, so a group's color replaces any other color the cell had.
